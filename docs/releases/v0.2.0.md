## What's new

1. **Real gyro aiming.** Open **Controls**, pick **Gyro** and set it to **Mouse** or **Xbox right stick**. It
   **calibrates itself**: hold the controller still or set it down for a second and any drift is learned away.
   **Turn with** *Either* (recommended) turns left and right however you hold the controller; *Yaw* and *Roll* are
   there too. New sliders: **Up and down speed**, **Fine aim** (slower small movements, full speed fast turns) and
   **Steady hands** (calms tiny shakes, never slows real movement). **Pause while** stops the gyro while you hold or
   touch something, for example your thumb on the right stick.
2. **Trackpad haptics.** Little ticks under your thumb as the trackball cursor rolls, one per scroll notch, and a click
   when a d-pad trackpad lands on a new direction. Set the strength in **Settings** > **Rumble & haptics** (and try
   **Feel trackpad haptics**); turn them off per pad in **Controls**.
3. **Battery on the tray icon.** A level bar shows the controller's charge: amber at 20 %, red at 10 %. Hover the icon
   for the exact number. You can turn it off in **Settings** > **Startup & tray**.
4. **USB-C cable and Bluetooth** are recognised and named on the Overview page. When the cable and the puck are both
   connected, BogiHub uses the cable.

**New and not yet tested on every setup:** gyro, trackpad haptics, the USB-C cable and Bluetooth. If something feels
wrong (haptics on the wrong pad, gyro turning oddly, the cable not working), please
[open an issue](https://github.com/OppaiMaster7/BogiHub/issues/new/choose).

## Updating

**Installer:** run the new installer. It closes BogiHub for you and keeps your settings and profiles.

**ZIP:** exit BogiHub from its tray menu, extract the new ZIP into a new folder and open the new **BogiHub.exe**.
Settings, profiles and **Start with Windows** move over by themselves.
